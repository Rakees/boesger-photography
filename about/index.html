Hello, I'm Phillip Bösger

Born in 1997, I'm a German photographer passionate about capturing life's authentic moments around the world.

I work as a remote software developer, travel the world as a digital nomad, and want to reach people with my photography.

My Photography Journey

📸 Since 2020

Since 2020, I have been actively pursuing photography as a hobby, focusing on street photography, events, and weddings for friends. Driven by my travels and the urge to discover something new, I have journeyed extensively in recent years to capture the world's moments. Equipped with my Sony Alpha 7 III, a variety of lenses, filters, and refined technical skills, I am well-prepared for many shooting scenarios.

📍 Street Photography

Capturing authentic moments in urban environments and discovering stories in everyday life.

🎉 Events & Weddings

Documenting special moments and celebrations for friends, creating memories that last a lifetime.

🌍 Travel Photography

Exploring the world with my Sony Alpha 7 III, capturing diverse cultures and breathtaking landscapes.
